# Cosmic Crisp — NASA Space Apps Challenge 2-Minute Pitch & Live Farmer Demo

**Project:** Cosmic Crisp — Space-to-Soil Crop, Irrigation & Pest Resilience Advisor  
**Target Pitch Window:** 90–120 seconds (~270 spoken words at ~140 wpm) + 60–90 second live product walkthrough  
**Hero Case Study:** Rajesh, a wine-grape grower in **Nashik, Maharashtra, India** facing an El Niño dry spell

---

## Part 1: Timed 2-Minute Pitch Script (Word-for-Word)

### `[0:00 – 0:25]` The Story & The "Why"
> Meet Rajesh, a vineyard grower in Nashik, Maharashtra, India. Before the monsoon arrives, he has to make two high-stakes bets: stretching shrinking well water across dry months, and choosing sprays before pests wipe out his canopy.  
> Under **El Niño**, Maharashtra gets ~**24% less rain** with clear, hot skies. That flips the field upside down: fungal downy mildew fades, while sap-sucking **thrips, mealybugs, and mites** explode—and rural apps fail once cell signal drops.

### `[0:25 – 0:55]` The Space-to-Soil Breakthrough
> That is why we built **Cosmic Crisp**. Instead of generic almanacs, Cosmic Crisp fuses **NASA POWER** climate normals, **NASA SMAP and GPM** moisture, **NASADEM** slope, and **ISRIC SoilGrids** chemistry with **NOAA's live Oceanic Niño Index** and **51-member 6-month seasonal forecasts**.  
> Within seconds, orbital telemetry powers decisions across **88 FAO EcoCrop species** inside an offline-first Progressive Web App that persists farm plans locally without connectivity.

### `[0:55 – 1:30]` Solutions & Measurable Farmer Impact
> Cosmic Crisp links three field decisions on one farm profile:  
> First, **Crop Rotation & ROI**, matching soil nutrients and slope to resilient crops.  
> Second, **Water-Scarcity Irrigation**, balancing 6-month evapotranspiration against rainfall—recommending low-head **Rain Pipes** at **82% efficiency** over **54% furrow**, with storm-coiling contingencies.  
> Third, **Climate-Pathology Pest Strategy**, alerting Rajesh *before* bud-break that clear El Niño skies lower **Grape Downy Mildew** while spiking **Thrips**, paired with a synced **Integrated Pest Management** checklist.

### `[1:30 – 1:55]` Transition into the Live Farmer Demo
> Cosmic Crisp turns orbital foresight into offline-ready action before losses hit the canopy.  
> Let's step into Rajesh's boots and watch how a farmer plans an entire El Niño season in ninety seconds.

## Part 2: Synchronized 5-Step Live Farmer Demo Walkthrough (~90 seconds)

Follow the exact flow of the pitch story inside the running **Cosmic Crisp** app.

### Step 1 — Ground the Farm in Space & Soil (`Overview` Tab · ~15s)
- **Click:** Open **New Farm** in the workspace toolbar, name it `Nashik Vineyard`, search `Nashik, Maharashtra, India`, and click **Create** (or select the saved farm from the **Farm Profile** dropdown).
- **Set Crop:** Under **Your Farm Inputs**, select **Wine Grapes** (*Vitis vinifera*).
- **Say to Judges:** *"The moment Rajesh picks his parcel, Cosmic Crisp streams live NASA climate normals, SoilGrids pH and clay/sand texture, and terrain slope—automatically saving a dedicated profile for his farm."*

### Step 2 — Check Crop Economics & Field Readiness (`Overview` Tab · ~15s)
- **Point to:** The **Satellite, Terrain & Soil** card and **Top Statistical Recommendation** hero panel.
- **Say to Judges:** *"Even before looking months ahead, Rajesh sees his root moisture, hardiness zone, soil nitrogen balance, and rotation ROI if he diversifies inter-rows or adjacent blocks."*

### Step 3 — Solve El Niño Water Scarcity with Rain Pipes (`Irrigation` Tab · ~25s)
- **Click:** Select **Irrigation** in the top navigation bar.
- **Controls to Toggle:**
  1. Show the live **NOAA ONI** readout at the top right.
  2. Click **El Niño Stress** under the ENSO scenario bar.
  3. Click **Severe Scarcity** under Water Supply / Scarcity.
- **Point to:** The **6-Month Rainfall vs. Crop ETc** bar chart and **5-Method Decision Comparison** ranking **Rain Pipes (Perforated Micro-Spray)** (82% efficiency) and **Drip / SDI** (92%) far above **Furrow** (54%).
- **Say to Judges:** *"With El Niño cutting monsoon rain, Cosmic Crisp quantifies Rajesh's 6-month deficit and ranks low-head Rain Pipes at the top—cutting evaporation waste while letting him roll up laterals before unexpected P90 storm bursts."*

### Step 4 — Anticipate the El Niño Fungal-to-Insect Shift (`Pest & Disease` Tab · ~25s)
- **Click:** Select **Pest & Disease** in the top navigation bar.
- **Show Cross-Sync:** Point out that **Wine Grapes**, **Nashik**, **El Niño Stress**, and **Rain Pipes** are already synced automatically, with **IPM Balance** active by default.
- **Point to:**
  1. The **6-Month ENSO Fungal vs. Insect Risk Outlook** chart: **Insect & Mite Risk** (amber bars) leads while **Fungal & Mildew Risk** (blue bars) stays suppressed under clearer, drier skies.
  2. Click **Chilli & Grape Thrips** or **Vine & Pink Hibiscus Mealybug** under **Area-Sourced Threats** to show early scouting signs, blue sticky trap prep, biologicals (*Beauveria bassiana* / Neem NSKE 5%), and how switching the **Synced Irrigation Method** dropdown from `Rain Pipes` to `Sprinkler` immediately shifts fungal leaf-wetness risk upward.
- **Say to Judges:** *"Because clear El Niño weather suppresses Downy Mildew but drives thrips and mites, Rajesh stages blue sticky traps and Neem bio-controls weeks before symptoms appear—instead of wasting money on unneeded fungicides."*

### Step 5 — Prove Offline Rural Resilience (`Workspace Toolbar` · ~10s)
- **Click:** Toggle **Field Offline Test** in the top toolbar, then check off **Deploy sticky cards & pheromone traps** in the **Pre-Season Protection & Readiness Checklist**.
- **Say to Judges:** *"Out in a zero-signal vineyard, Cosmic Crisp keeps running from cached satellite packs and queues Rajesh's checklist changes locally until signal returns. Space-grade intelligence, built for the last mile."*
